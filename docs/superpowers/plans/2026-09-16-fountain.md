# Mermaid fountain and water impact

**Goal:** Replace the placeholder figure and decorative ripple rings with a green bronze mermaid sculpture, gravity-driven water and waves in the actual pool surface.

**Design:** Keep the existing pool footprint and pedestal placement. The user’s mermaid reference replaces the earlier modern-loop direction: a seated mermaid with a curled scaled tail, broad fins, raised arm, sculpted face and flowing hair stands in a shell basin on a textured stone base. Generated green bronze colour and derived bump/normal/roughness maps provide cast-metal patina; basin water exits a projecting outlet. Water follows a ballistic trajectory, thins as it accelerates, breaks into droplets near impact, and produces short-lived upward splash droplets. The impact applies a bounded, volume-conserving force to the existing damped wave simulation; no separate ripple meshes remain. Photo mode freezes the actual current stream, splash and wave geometry for a stable exposure.

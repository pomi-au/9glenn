Dimension audit

The drawing uses straight walls, geometric curves and editable SVG text. One SVG coordinate equals one millimetre. The original PDF is available only as a comparison reference.

Verified scope: the checks below compare reconstructed wall-face spans and level heights with legible printed dimensions. They do not certify every detail or a 100% image match.

Source comparison: the overlay uses the overall scan scale, without deforming the PDF to fit the model. Local scan distortion and differences remain visible.

Ruler: distances are calculated from SVG geometry. Free point placements show approximate values; recognised dimension endpoints show the dimensioned model span. Exports contain vectors and text, without the reference image.
